12 questions before you choose your bucket list

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to the goals that will actually stick.

  1. What have I always said I would do 'one day'?
  2. What would I regret not doing if I could not do it after next year?
  3. Which experience have I already done that I would happily repeat?
  4. What do I want to be able to tell my grandchildren I did?
  5. Which item on my list is really someone else's dream?
  6. Where do I want to wake up on my next birthday?
  7. Who do I want to share my best experiences with?
  8. What scares me a little, in a good way?
  9. What would I do with a free week and a small budget?
  10. What is the cheapest thing on my list that I could do this month?
  11. Which skill would make my life richer if I learned it?
  12. If I could cross off just three things in 2027, which would they be?

Make your bucket list board in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: write 30 things. Empty your head without judging. Big trips, small treats, skills, people to see.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below. Short, honest answers are enough; nobody else reads them.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three for 2027. Not thirty. Choose three, one big, one cheap and one with another person, and give each a month.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your first item and its first step into a phone lock screen, so you see it every day and act on it, not only add it to a list.

Questions

What should I put on a bucket list?

Put things you would regret not doing: places, skills, people and small joys. Mix big and cheap items so you can tick something off every month. This page has 36 ideas in 6 groups (adventure and travel, learn and create, body and challenge, people and memories, give back and grow, everyday joy), each with a first step for this week.

How do I turn a bucket list into a vision board?

Pick three items, write each as a short sentence with a month, add one first step, and put it where you see it every day. A phone lock screen works best because you see it dozens of times a day.

How many items should a bucket list have?

A list of 30 to 100 is fine to dream with, but pick only three for the year. Fewer items get done more often.

Why do most bucket lists never get done?

Items without a date or a first step stay wishes. Give every item a month and a step you can do this week, and keep the list visible.
